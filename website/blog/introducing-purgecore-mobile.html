Introducing PurgeCore Mobile

2026

PurgeCore has been a Windows-only tool since it started. PurgeCore Mobile is a separate app for Android, built for what a phone actually needs rather than a port of Windows features that don't apply there.

What it does

Two things: a storage overview showing used and free space at a glance, and a duplicate photo and video finder. Duplicates are matched by content, not filename, so a renamed copy is still caught, and at least one copy is always kept, a group can never be wiped out by a single action. Deletion goes through Android's own system confirmation dialog, nothing happens silently.

Why not more features from the Windows app

Registry cleanup, a startup manager, driver checks, none of that exists on Android, or means something different there. Rather than force a Windows-shaped tool onto a platform it doesn't fit, PurgeCore Mobile started with the two things that make sense on a phone specifically.

Free, with ads

No paywall, no premium tier. Development is supported by a small banner ad, the same model most free Android apps use.

Not yet on Google Play, the APK is available to install directly for now.
