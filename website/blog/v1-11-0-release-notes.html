PurgeCore v1.11.0: Battery Health

2026

A new tab for laptop users: a one-click way to check how much your battery has degraded over time.

New: Battery Health

PurgeCore now has a dedicated Battery tab in the sidebar. One click generates Windows' own battery diagnostic report, design capacity versus current full-charge capacity, and usage history over time, then opens it in your browser. No reimplementation of battery diagnostics, just a fast path to the report Windows already knows how to build.

The tab only appears on devices that report having a battery, so desktop PC users won't see it cluttering their sidebar.

If your laptop feels like it's not holding a charge like it used to, this is the quickest way to find out whether that's actually true.
