PurgeCore Mobile: How It's Funded

2026

PurgeCore Mobile is free, with no premium tier and nothing locked behind a purchase. Development is funded by a single banner ad shown at the bottom of the screen, below the app's own content.

What that means in practice

One ad unit, always in the same place, never covering content or interrupting a scan. No video ads that play automatically, no full-screen interstitials between actions, no rewarded-ad mechanics tied to using a feature. It's there, it's small, and it funds the rest of the app staying free.

What doesn't change because of it

The ad network sees standard ad request data, the same as any app using it, PurgeCore Mobile itself doesn't send your photos, your storage details, or anything about how you use the app anywhere. Duplicate detection still happens entirely on your device.

If a single banner ad still isn't your thing, offering a coffee on the website supports development directly instead.
